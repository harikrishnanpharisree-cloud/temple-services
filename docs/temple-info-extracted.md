- **Address:** Panayakkal Devaswom, Kattikkunnu, Chembu P.O., Vaikom – 686608
- **Phone:** 8086469089, 9446552071

**Facilities / terms (translated):**

- Can be used for wedding engagements, weddings, tea parties, birthdays, memorial rites (adiyanthiram), meetings, and cultural programs.
- Hall area: 500 sq. ft.
- Well ventilated and naturally lit interior.
- 5 restrooms and a kitchen/cooking area.
- 3-phase electrical connection.
- 2 parking areas (temple grounds and the front of the auditorium).
- 3 access routes to reach the auditorium.
- Permission to conduct "Mangalyam charthal" (thali-tying wedding rite) at the Devi's sanctum on pooja days.
- Low rental rate: ₹15,000.
- Non-vegetarian food is permitted.
